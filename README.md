## Samruddhi Kakade

AI Engineer · Independent Researcher · Philadelphia, PA

I am an AI engineer and independent researcher focused on evaluating large language models. My research examines
how models behave during tasks, whether repeated runs agree, and how reliable evaluation judgements are. Since
January 2025 I have worked as an AI engineer on projects for Johnson & Johnson, developing data-ingestion pipelines
for retrieval-augmented healthcare chatbots. I earned a Master of Artificial Intelligence and a B.S. in Computer
Science from Illinois Institute of Technology in May 2024.

### Research

**[Reading the Board, Playing Another One: Turn-Level Evaluation of Open LLM Agents on Textual Sokoban](https://samruddhikakade.github.io/research/reading-the-board/)**<br>
Sole author · Preprint, 7 October 2026 · [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23220308)

Evaluated five open language-model agents of 3 to 8B parameters on textual Sokoban turn by turn, over 180 episodes
and 1,561 steps. Induced a failure taxonomy from blind reading passes and reported its annotator agreement, human
label checks and a null validity test.
